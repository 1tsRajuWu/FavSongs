one link

The link is the shelf.

Send it in a chat, drop it in a bio, paste it in a message. Whoever opens it sees the same songs, in the same order.

  • Nothing to sign up for. Whoever opens the link sees the same songs, in the same order, with no account wall on the other side.
  • Two shapes. A short link from our backend when it is reachable, and a long self-contained link that always works — even offline.
  • Artwork and previews. Rows resolve real covers and 30-second previews in the listener's browser.
  • Optional cloud. Sign in with Google and your shelf follows you between devices; keep it private or list it in Discover.

how it works

Three moves.

01 — save

Keep your favourites

Add a title and artist, or paste a track link and let the details fill themselves in.

02 — order

Make it read like a mood

Your shelf keeps its order. The first song becomes the pinned favourite on the shared view.

03 — share

Send one link

Copy the link and hand it over. They can listen, open each song, or save the whole shelf to their own.
