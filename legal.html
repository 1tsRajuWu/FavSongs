Legal

FavSongs is a shelf that lives in your browser. This page says plainly what that means for your data.

Privacy Policy

Your shelf is stored in your own browser (localStorage) under the key favsongs.shelf.v1. It is not uploaded anywhere unless you sign in.

If you sign in with Google, we store your display name, avatar URL, the songs on your shelf, and the time of your last sign-in — enough to sync the shelf and show it to you on another device. Your email address stays in the authentication record and is never copied into our tables.

Share links carry the songs inside the URL itself. A short link stores only the song list (title, artist, album, artwork URL, listen URL) against a random ID. There are no analytics, no trackers and no advertising on this site.

Song pages fetch public data directly from third parties in your browser: the iTunes Search API (artwork and 30-second previews), lyrics.ovh (lyrics), the Spotify and YouTube oEmbed endpoints (importing a link) and SimpleIcons (service logos). Those providers see a normal web request from your browser and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Terms of Use

Use FavSongs to share music you love. Don't use share links for spam, harassment or anything unlawful, and don't present someone else's shelf as your own. Songs, artwork and previews belong to their rights holders; opening a track hands you to the platform that hosts it, under that platform's terms.

Shelves you list in Discover are public. You can unlist one at any time from your shelf. The service is provided as-is, without warranties, and may change or stop.
